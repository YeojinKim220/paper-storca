# Result (Figure 5)

## stORCA types and locates each change, beyond what DE calls

A call says that the spatial expression of a gene differs between conditions more than it differs between replicate sections. A biologist then asks three further questions. What kind of change is it: a new shape, or the same shape at a different strength? Where in the tissue does it occur? And would a simpler test have found it? stORCA answers the first question with a factorization of ROS into a pattern term, $\rho_W$, and an amplitude term, $\kappa$ (Methods). The second question is answered by the residual of the matched expression fields, which can be mapped and tested against tissue annotations. For the third, we compared calls with two tests run on the same sections. Pseudobulk differential expression (DE) asks whether the total expression changes, in the whole section or within one domain. DESpace2 asks whether the condition changes expression differently between domains, so a change shared by every domain lies outside its question. We first checked the factorization on changes of known kind and then applied all three readings to five replicate comparisons (Fig. 5).

The factorization separated amplitude from pattern when the kind of change was known (Fig. 5a). In the amplitude series, ten genes kept one spatial template while the contrast between conditions grew from 0 to 2, with three sections per condition. As the contrast grew, the median $\kappa$ over the nine between-condition pairs fell from 1.00 to 0.99, 0.96, 0.88 and 0.76, while $\rho_W$ stayed at 0.999 or above. From a contrast of 0.5 onward, the amplitude term carried at least 97% of the loss. In the pattern series, we fixed the correspondence and randomized the shape of each gene without changing its strength. To do so, we flipped the sign of each spatial eigenmode with probability *p*, so the expected $\rho_W$ is $1-2p$. Over 36,000 randomized profiles, $\kappa$ was 1.000 in every case, and the mean $\rho_W$ followed $1-2p$ from 1.00 to $-1.00$. Single profiles varied around this mean; at *p* = 0.3, the 5th to 95th percentiles of $\rho_W$ spanned $-0.47$ to 0.96. The maps show the two kinds of change on one gene (Fig. 5a, right).

We applied the factorization to five comparisons in four datasets, each with replicate sections in both conditions. ARTISTA follows axolotl telencephalon regeneration with Stereo-seq; we compared three sections at 2 days post injury (DPI) with three at 20 DPI, giving nine between-condition and six within-condition pairs. NRDR compares mouse primary visual cortex (V1) of normally reared and dark-reared animals at postnatal day 28 with MERFISH, two animals per condition. Each animal gave one posterior and one anterior section. Two sections of one animal are not independent, so we analyzed the two positions as separate two-against-two designs. HPOA compares the hypothalamic preoptic area of three naive males with three males that attacked pups, imaged with MERFISH. The colitis comparison contrasts three healthy colon slices with three slices at day 9 of dextran sulfate sodium colitis. The slices of one condition come from one mouse, so they are technical replicates. stORCA tested 775, 377, 362, 148 and 480 genes and called 113, 32, 11, 11 and 386 (one-sided limma, BH-adjusted *P* < 0.05). For each between-condition pair we computed the amplitude share $s$ of the loss in concordance. A pair was typed pattern when $s<1/3$ or $\rho_W\le0$, amplitude when $s>2/3$, and mixed otherwise. Each gene took the type that occurred most often among its between-condition pairs. The type describes a call; it does not enter the test.

Calls covered all three types, in proportions that differed between datasets (Fig. 5b). Pattern calls were the largest group in ARTISTA (59 pattern, 44 mixed, 10 amplitude) and in the colitis comparison (160, 157, 69). In NRDR posterior, pattern and mixed calls were equal (14, 14, 4). Amplitude calls were the largest group in HPOA (2, 3, 6) and NRDR anterior (1, 4, 6), both with 11 calls. The between-condition type could include changes that replicate sections already show. We therefore also typed the excess of the between-condition pairs over the within-condition pairs. This kept the type of 84 of 113 calls in ARTISTA (74%), 21 of 32 in NRDR posterior (66%) and 270 of 386 in colitis (70%). In NRDR anterior and HPOA, 10 and 8 of 11 calls kept their type. Most calls that moved went toward amplitude; amplitude calls in ARTISTA rose from 10 to 29.

Two ARTISTA calls show what each type looks like on the tissue (Fig. 5b, right). *CDKN1B* was typed amplitude ($\kappa$ 0.24, $\rho_W$ 0.74, $s$ 0.82; BH-adjusted *P* = 5 × 10<sup>−9</sup>). Its expression rose along the ventricular band of both hemispheres at 20 DPI, and its spatial structure became stronger ($h$ = 2.11). The residual rose by a similar amount in all four compartments, with medians over the nine between-condition pairs of 0.14 to 0.17. *FABP7* was typed pattern ($\kappa$ 0.96, $\rho_W$ 0.75, $s$ 0.12; BH-adjusted *P* = 2 × 10<sup>−6</sup>). Its increase concentrated in the dorsal half of the injured hemisphere, with a median residual of 0.68 against 0.39 to 0.48 in the other compartments. The injured dorsal compartment had the largest residual in all nine pairs. Global pseudobulk DE also calls both genes, so they illustrate the types, not detection beyond DE.

In NRDR posterior, the residual of 30 of the 32 calls concentrated in specific cortical layers (Fig. 5c). We drew layers on the four sections from the cell-type annotation and oriented each section with the pia up (Fig. 5c, left). For every call and between-condition pair, we marked cells where the absolute residual formed a local hotspot (local Moran's *I*, high-high, permutation *P* < 0.05). We then tested each layer for an excess of hotspot cells (hypergeometric test, BH over four layers). Hotspots covered a median of 5.5% of source cells per gene (2.8% to 13.2%). We counted a layer for a gene when it was enriched in at least three of the four between-condition pairs. L4 was enriched for 21 calls, L2/3 for 13, L5 for 4 and L6 for 2. By cell type, L4 neurons were enriched for 20 calls and L2/3 neurons for 19. *Stard8* changed in L2/3, where its band in normally reared sections weakened in dark-reared sections. *Trpc3* changed in L4, and *Arc* rose across L2/3 and L4. L4 receives the thalamic visual input and L2/3 its main cortical relay, so the residuals sit in the layers expected to respond to visual deprivation.

Most stORCA calls without a DE or DESpace2 call were pattern changes, whereas the calls shared with pseudobulk DE included most amplitude changes (Fig. 5d). In ARTISTA, DESpace2 called 24 of the 113 stORCA calls. Of the other 89, 60 were called by pseudobulk DE and 29 by neither test. In NRDR posterior, DESpace2 called no gene with layers as domains. Pseudobulk DE called 13 of the 32 stORCA calls, leaving 19 called by neither. NRDR anterior had 2 such calls of 11, and HPOA none of 11. Of the 29 ARTISTA calls without a DE call, 22 were pattern, 7 mixed and none amplitude; of the 19 in NRDR, 11 were pattern, 7 mixed and 1 amplitude. The calls shared with DE held all 10 ARTISTA amplitude calls (37 pattern, 37 mixed, 10 amplitude). An amplitude change alters totals, which pseudobulk DE measures, whereas a pattern change can move expression while keeping the total. The overlap also runs the other way. Pseudobulk DE alone called 165 genes in ARTISTA and 19 in NRDR posterior, and DESpace2 called 30 ARTISTA genes that stORCA did not. A change of level without a change of spatial structure is removed by the centering in ROS, so these genes are not spatial changes in the sense tested here.

Named NRDR genes show the calls that the other tests did not make at two sections per condition (Fig. 5e). Two calls were layer DE but not global DE or DESpace2 calls. *Stard8* (stORCA BH-adjusted *P* = 0.027) had a weaker L2/3 band in both dark-reared sections; global DE gave FDR 0.066, layer DE 0.025 and DESpace2 0.52. *Scube1* (amplitude, 0.032) kept its L4 peak at lower strength; global DE gave 0.060, layer DE 0.027 and DESpace2 0.999. Three calls were not DE in any layer. *Prkca* (0.044) was high in L2/3 and L4 of both normally reared sections and fell to the L5 level in both dark-reared sections. Global DE gave FDR 0.17, the smallest layer FDR was 0.10, and DESpace2 gave 0.999. *Trpc3* (0.040) had a weaker L4 band (global 0.40, layer 0.11, DESpace2 0.999). *Slc24a3* (pattern, 0.027) had a flatter L4 peak (global 0.22, layer 0.052, DESpace2 0.60). In each case the change appears in both animals of each condition, and the layer means show the same contrast.

The direction of the NRDR calls agrees with the source study (Fig. 5f). That study reported higher expression of immediate early genes in dark-reared V1. stORCA called 7 of 11 activity genes, and all 7 had a stronger spatial structure in dark-reared sections ($h$ 0.63 to 1.38). Across the 11 genes, $h$ agreed in sign with the pseudobulk log<sub>2</sub> fold change for all 11, and the two correlated with Pearson *r* = 0.90. On the excess over within-condition pairs, three calls were amplitude (*Fos*, *Fosb*, *Junb*), three mixed and one pattern (*Nr4a2*). This is consistent with a rise in strength more than a change of shape. The layer maps also point to a change in laminar organization. In the L4 band of dark-reared sections, 14% to 16% of neurons carried the L2/3 label, against 5% to 7% in normally reared sections. The two ranges did not overlap across the eight sections. We report this boundary change as a candidate, because the layers were drawn from the same cell-type labels.

The interpretation has limits that the numbers make explicit. The NRDR examples are not changes that a layer test cannot see. In L4, both normally reared sections of *Prkca* and *Trpc3* lie above both dark-reared sections (nominal *P* = 0.006 and 0.008). Correction over 2,000 layer tests needed *P* < 0.0019. When the anterior sections were added (four sections per condition), pseudobulk DE called 15 of the 19 posterior non-DE calls, including both genes. The measured claim is that stORCA called these changes at two sections per condition, where the DE tests did not. In ARTISTA, the 29 calls without a DE call are sparse genes whose maps show no difference visible by eye, so ARTISTA supports the count, not an example. The type summarizes the between-condition pairs; 66% to 91% of calls keep it on the excess over replicates. Finally, the colitis replicates are technical, so its 386 calls are not comparable in number with the other comparisons.
