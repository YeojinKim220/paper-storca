## Statistical design and ROS properties

| A reviewer can say | Answer | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Isn't clipping ROS to [0, 2] ad hoc, or `Moran I > 0.03` pre-filtering arbitrary? | Zero-diagonal Moran weights exclude self-weight single-cell shot noise at the cost of indefiniteness. Direct comparison against the naturally [0, 2]-bounded PSD operator $W^\top W$ injects shot noise into numerator and denominator, eliminating calls in HPOA (11 -> 0) and NRDR (32 -> 0). `Moran I > 0.03` is label-free independent filtering (Bourgon et al. 2010), and clipping preserves 94 to 100% of calls while bounding extreme small-denominator fluctuations. | Zero-diagonal vs lag-operator benchmark comparison (Methods) |
| Why can forward (`A -> B`) and reverse (`B -> A`) directions call different genes (e.g., HPOA)? | Under registration-free soft transport, projecting target expression onto a reference graph introduces intrinsic attenuation ($\alpha^2 < 1$), so `A -> B` tests loss of source patterns (`naive > aggr`, 11/11 calls) whereas `B -> A` tests emerging target patterns (`aggr > naive`, 17/20 calls). Both remain null-calibrated (`null_max = 0`), and bidirectional p-value combination or symmetric formulations are documented. | HPOA directional call analysis (Methods) |
| A 3 vs 3 replicate design gives only 9 false labelings and correlated pairs. | 3 vs 3 matches standard spatial transcriptomics cohort sizes, complemented by replicate-scaling checks (2 vs 2, 3 vs 3, 6 vs 6) and consistency across 4 to 6 independent datasets. Primary significance calls come from moderated empirical Bayes limma, and exhaustive sample-label swaps verify that pair non-independence does not inflate false positives (0 calls on LIBD, HPOA, NRDR). | Sample-label permutation diagnostics across cohorts (Methods) |
| In Cortex 3 dpi, CODA and stORCA show closer lesion-only marker fractions than in Colitis. | Reactive glial markers in Cortex genuinely rise across shared tissue outside the lesion (`shared` markers), whereas in Colitis D0 vs D9, where an entire epithelial compartment disappears, unmatched-epithelium overlay fills registration and domain competitor top lists across all 9 section pairs. | Cross-study marker comparison (Fig. 4) |

## Benchmark cohorts and robustness

| A reviewer can say | Answer | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Partial OT's zero-cost dummy cell can drop intact source cells when target tissue gains a novel compartment. | This reflects matching shared mass $\min(\sum a_i, \sum b_j)$ cleanly without forced distortion. stORCA reports per-cell retained fractions $r_i$ and spatial retention maps so users inspect excluded regions directly (Methods). | Partial OT retention diagnostics (Fig. 4; Methods) |
| SysVI hyperparameters (`vamp` prior, `kl_weight = 1`, `z_distance_cycle_weight = 5`) might be tuned per dataset. | `prior = "vamp"` is the scvi-tools default and `kl_weight = 1, z_distance_cycle_weight = 5` come directly from the official scvi-tools SysVI tutorial, applied unchanged across all datasets. | Shared SysVI configuration (Methods) |
| Reusing one thinning seed and drawing 50 stochastic matchings introduces Monte Carlo variance. | Empirical resampling variance across matching seeds is 26 to 155x smaller than biological replicate variance (Fig. 3), making matching-seed effects on limma calls negligible. | Matching-seed stability benchmark (Fig. 3) |
| Time-course cohorts are excluded from the main two-condition claims. | Sequencing depth varies 2 to 3x across developmental time points and effect magnitude can vary non-monotonically under shape changes, so main claims focus on well-posed two-condition comparisons. | Time-course cohort scope assessment |
